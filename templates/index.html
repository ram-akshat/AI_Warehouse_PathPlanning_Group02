SEARCH PROBLEM

Model and methods

State and actions

The state is the robot’s row and column. It starts at packing and finishes at dispatch. Each move enters one adjacent cell. Shelves and restricted zones block entry.

Costs and heuristic

Ordinary cells cost 1 and crowded cells cost 4. Manhattan distance is a lower bound because each move closes at most one unit of that distance and costs at least 1.

Obstacle discovery

A* plans on the known map. When a trolley blocks the next cell, the robot stops, marks that cell as blocked, and reruns A* from its current position.
